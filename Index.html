03

How to read this portfolio

Each campaign has one clear goal. This is what each goal means in plain English.

📣AwarenessGoal: show the brand to as many new, relevant people as possible. Measured by Reach, the number of different people who saw the ad.
💜EngagementGoal: grow the brand’s Facebook and Instagram community with new likes and followers. Measured by Followers & likes.
💬Leads & MessagingGoal: get interested customers to message the brand directly (account opening, enquiries). Measured by Conversations.
🔗TrafficGoal: bring people to the brand’s profile or WhatsApp channel. Measured by Visits.
ReachThe number of unique people who saw the ad. One person is counted once.
ImpressionsThe total number of times the ad appeared on a screen. One person can see it more than once, so this is higher than Reach.
FrequencyThe average number of times each person saw the ad (for example 2.85x).
Cost per resultWhat one result costs: one follow, one conversation, or reaching 1,000 people. Lower is better.

07

What the numbers tell us

Patterns we found across all campaigns.

Engagement is our most affordable goal

New followers cost between Rs 1.60 and Rs 11.42 each. The best result: Rang e Zar at Rs 1.60, followed by MMK at Rs 2.35 across 130,015 followers.

Awareness is steady and predictable

Reaching 1,000 people cost Rs 8.29 to Rs 18.01. Rang e Zar and MMK delivered the lowest cost.

Lead cost depends on intent

A conversation cost Rs 17.55 to Rs 93.72. Account-opening campaigns usually sit at the higher end (Rs 44 to Rs 78) because they target people ready to take a serious step.

WhatsApp channel drives cheap traffic

41,922 visits at just Rs 1.81 each, a low-cost way to build a direct line to the audience.

Recommended next steps
Scale what worksIncrease budget on the campaigns with the lowest cost per result, such as the Rang e Zar and MMK engagement campaigns.
Turn fans into customersShow lead and account-opening ads to people who already follow or engage with the brand.
Refresh weaker creativesTest new images, videos and messages on campaigns that cost more than the average for their goal.
